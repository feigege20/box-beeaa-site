IATA vs IMO Shipping: B2B Case Transport Regulations 2026

Air vs sea freight case regulations. IATA dangerous goods, IMO IMDG, testing, marking.

International B2B case shipping requires IATA (air) and IMO IMDG (sea) compliance. This guide compares 8 dimensions to help B2B buyers navigate complex regulations.
