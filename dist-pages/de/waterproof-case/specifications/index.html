Frequently Asked Questions

  1. What's the difference between IP67 and IP68? IP67 = dust tight + temporary immersion (1m/30min). IP68 = dust tight + continuous immersion (3m/manufacturer-specified). IP69K = high-pressure hot water jets.
  2. Can your cases handle saltwater submersion? Yes, our IP68-rated cases are tested for continuous saltwater submersion up to 3m depth. Perfect for marine and diving applications.
  3. What's the operating temperature range? Standard cases: -40 to +120°C. Special low-temperature models available down to -60°C for arctic expeditions.
  4. Do you offer pressure equalization valves? Yes, our WP-400 and larger cases include pressure equalization valves for altitude changes (air transport, mountain expeditions).
