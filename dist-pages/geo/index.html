Global Sourcing for OEM Protective Cases

Country-specific sourcing guides for the world's largest B2B protective case markets.

KeXinMaterials has supplied OEM/ODM protective cases since 2014. This page lists our country-specific guides covering regulatory compliance, industry focus, shipping terms, and FAQs for the top 5 B2B markets outside China.

Country Sourcing Guides

  • Germany — EU REACH/RoHS, DIN tolerances, Bundeswehr & automotive Tier-1. FOB Ningbo to Hamburg/Bremerhaven 14 days.
  • United Kingdom — Post-Brexit UKCA & REACH, DEF STAN 81-41, offshore & broadcast. FOB Ningbo to Felixstowe 28-32 days.
  • United States — Berry Amendment, MIL-STD-810G, Section 301 tariffs, DOD/federal. FOB Ningbo to Long Beach/Oakland 16-20 days.
  • Australia — RCM, ISPM 15 biosecurity, mining & ADF. FOB Ningbo to Fremantle 18-22 days / Sydney 22-26 days.
  • Japan — JIS precision (±0.2mm), PSE marking, electronics & robotics. FOB Ningbo to Yokohama 4-5 days.

Trade Terms We Support

  • EXW Shenzhen — Buyer picks up at our 18,000㎡ facility in Guangdong
  • FOB Ningbo — Container loaded at Ningbo-Zhoushan port, buyer takes responsibility at the ship's rail

We do not offer DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) or any customs-cleared door delivery. Importers in destination countries handle their own VAT, customs duties, and final-mile logistics. This is standard B2B factory-direct practice and avoids hidden costs in our pricing.
