MIL-SPEC Certification Guide: MIL-STD-810H, 461, 464 Explained

What MIL-SPEC means, which standards apply, how to verify, when you need it.

Step 1: Understand MIL-STD-810H

Environmental testing for military equipment. Covers: drop (Method 516.8), vibration (514.8), temperature (-40°C to +70°C, 501.7/502.7), humidity (507.6), rain (506.5), sand/dust (510.7), immersion (512.7), shock (27G, 516.8).

Step 2: Understand MIL-STD-461

EMI/RFI shielding for electronic equipment. Frequency range: 10 kHz to 40 GHz. Shielding effectiveness: 60-100 dB for military applications. Required for radio, encrypted comms, sensitive electronics.

Step 3: Determine Required Standards

Tactical field use: 810H Method 506.5 (rain) + 512.7 (immersion). Military aircraft: 810H + 461. Ground vehicle: 810H + vibration spec. Naval: 810H + salt fog (509.7).

Step 4: Verify Supplier Test Reports

Request accredited lab test reports: SGS, TUV Rheinland, BV, Intertek. Reports should include: standard reference, test date, pass/fail criteria, photos.

Step 5: Sample Validation Testing

Test 3-5 units in your facility. IP rating: water immersion test. Drop: 1.5-2.4m onto concrete. Vibration: simulate transport. Field test: actual use case for 30 days.

Step 6: Maintain Documentation

Keep all test reports, certifications, QC records for 5-7 years. Required for defense contracts, government bids, military procurement. Some buyers require annual re-certification.
