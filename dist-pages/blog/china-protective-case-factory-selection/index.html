How to Select a China Protective Case Factory: 12-Point Checklist

By KeXinMaterials Sourcing Team | 2026-08-29

Vetting suppliers for OEM partnerships. Red flags and best practices.

Sourcing protective cases from China offers significant cost advantages, but selecting the right factory is critical for long-term success. Based on 12 years of B2B export experience, here's a comprehensive 12-point checklist.

Pre-Screening (3 Points)

1. Verify Business License: Confirm the factory is a registered legal entity with 3+ years operational history. Cross-check with China customs registration for export experience.

2. Check Factory Size: Minimum 5,000㎡ facility for serious OEM work. Smaller factories struggle with consistency and capacity. Visit or request live video tour.

3. Confirm Export Experience: Look for 5+ years exporting to your market. Request references from buyers in your country. Check Alibaba/Made-in-China/Globalsources reviews.

Quality Systems (3 Points)

4. ISO 9001 Certification: Verify current certificate (not expired). Confirm certification body is accredited (IAF member).

5. Product Certifications: For your market, verify: CE (EU), FCC (USA), ROHS (worldwide), REACH (EU), IP rating test reports (SGS/TUV/BV).

6. Quality Control Process: Request QC flowchart. Look for: incoming material inspection, in-process inspection, AQL final inspection, pre-shipment inspection. Reject factories that skip any step.

Production Capability (3 Points)

7. Equipment List: Minimum 30 injection molding machines (50T-2000T), 10+ assembly lines, foam cutting (CNC or waterjet), tooling workshop. Verify equipment photos and serial numbers.

8. Capacity & Lead Time: Confirm monthly capacity meets your needs (typically 5,000-50,000 cases/month for B2B). Standard lead time: 25-35 days for first order, 15-25 days for repeat orders.

9. R&D Capability: Look for in-house design team (5+ engineers), 3D CAD/CAM software, prototyping capability (3D printing, CNC machining), mold design experience.

Commercial (3 Points)

10. Payment Terms: Standard: 30% T/T deposit + 70% before shipment. Avoid 100% upfront. Use trade assurance (Alibaba) or LC for large orders.

11. Communication: English-speaking sales team, <12-hour response time, regular order updates (2x/week during production), willingness to do video calls.

12. After-Sales: Warranty policy (typically 1-2 years), defective unit replacement commitment, technical support for product issues, spare parts availability.

Red Flags to Avoid

• Factory refuses video tour or on-site visit

• No verifiable certifications or test reports

• Pressure for 100% payment upfront

• Significantly lower pricing than competitors (30%+ below market)

• No references from existing customers

• Sample quality differs from production quality (most common scam)

• Uses different brand name on invoice vs website

Best Practices

• Order samples first ($500-2,000 investment saves $50K+ mistakes)

• Start with small trial order (100-500 units) before scaling

• Use third-party inspection (SGS, BV, TUV) for orders $20K+

• Build relationship over 2-3 orders before considering exclusivity

• Visit factory annually to verify continued capability
