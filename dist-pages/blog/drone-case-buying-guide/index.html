Drone Case Buying Guide: DJI, Autel, Skydio Cases Compared

By KeXinMaterials Drone Solutions Team | 2026-08-29

Foam insert design, charging integration, transport scenarios.

Commercial drone operators invest $5,000-50,000+ in drone equipment. Proper protection cases are essential to protect this investment during transport, field operations, and storage. This guide compares case solutions for the top 3 commercial drone brands.

Why Drone Cases Matter

Drone damage costs: 35% occur during transport, 28% during field handling, 22% during storage, 15% during operation. A $150 case protects $5,000+ equipment, with 5-10x ROI typical. Drone cases also enable: organized accessory storage, faster field setup, professional appearance for client work.

DJI Drone Cases

Mavic 3 Series Case Requirements

Internal dimensions needed: 200x150x100mm (drone + controller + 3 batteries + accessories). Custom foam inserts required: precision-cut cavities for drone body, controller, batteries, propellers, charger, cables.

Popular cases: Hard shell ABS+PC, IP67 rated, 2.5kg empty weight, $95-180. TSA-approved locks for air travel. Pressure equalization valve for altitude changes.

DJI Matrice / Professional Cases

Larger cases needed: 500x400x250mm for Matrice 300/350 RTK. Heavy-duty construction, wheels for transport, $280-450. Custom foam for RTK base station, batteries, payload accessories.

Autel Drone Cases

EVO Lite / Lite+ Cases

Internal dimensions: 220x160x110mm. Foam design accommodates folding arms. Cases typically $110-220. Some include built-in charging integration via case-side ports.

EVO II Series Cases

Similar to DJI Mavic 3 dimensions. Cases $130-250. Rugged IP67 construction for field operations. Foam insert designs for accessories: 4-6 batteries, charging hub, smart controller, ND filters.

Skydio Drone Cases

Skydio 2/2+ Cases

Larger foam cavities needed due to Skydio's unique folding design. Cases $140-280. Often include custom compartment for Skydio controller with built-in screen.

Skydio X2 Cases

Enterprise-grade: 450x350x200mm internal. MIL-SPEC certification available for defense/security applications. $320-580. Custom foam for thermal camera, zoom camera, batteries, beacon.

Foam Insert Design Considerations

Material: Polyethylene (PE) or polyurethane (PU) foam, 30-50kg/m³ density. Layers: top convoluted (egg crate) foam for lid protection, middle custom-cut cavities, bottom flat foam.

Precision cutting: Waterjet (cleanest edges, $20-50/cavity), CNC router ($15-40/cavity), die-cut ($5-15/cavity for 100+ units). Custom design: 3D scan of equipment ensures perfect fit.

Charging Integration

Premium cases include: built-in charging hub (charge 3-4 batteries simultaneously), external power port (charge without opening case), battery level indicator, USB-C PD output for laptop/phone charging. Adds $50-150 to case cost but saves 30+ minutes per field session.

Air Transport Considerations

TSA-approved locks required for USA travel. Pressure equalization valve prevents vacuum lock at altitude. Battery storage: LiPo batteries must be in carry-on luggage (not checked), case should have dedicated battery compartment with fire-resistant liner. Lithium battery declaration required for air freight (UN3481 or UN3480).

Custom vs Off-the-Shelf

Off-the-shelf: $80-200, available immediately, fits "most" configurations, less protection. Custom OEM: $150-400, 30-45 day lead time, perfect fit, integrated charging, brand-specific. B2B recommendation: Custom for fleet > 10 drones, off-the-shelf for occasional use.
