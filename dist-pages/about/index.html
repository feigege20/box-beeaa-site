Our Story

KeXinMaterials (Guangdong) Co., Ltd. was founded in 2014 in Fusha Town, Zhongshan, Guangdong Province, China. What started as a 20-person plastic tool box workshop has grown into a 100+ employee, 13,000㎡ integrated manufacturing facility specializing in protective cases, tool boxes, military cases, drone cases, waterproof cases, and OEM/ODM custom projects.

Over 10 years, we have built 60+ injection molding machines, 20+ patents (including utility models and design patents), and an in-house IP67 testing lab certified to IEC 60529. We hold ISO9001:2015 quality management certification and supply to 50+ countries across North America, Europe, Asia, and the Middle East.
