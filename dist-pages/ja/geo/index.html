Why Source From China

KeXinMaterials has been supplying OEM/ODM protective cases since 2014 from our 13,000 m² factory in Guangdong. We support 6 product lines (drone, military, waterproof, tool, trolley, camera/medical) with full EN/ZH/DE/ES/FR/JA documentation. All shipments are EXW Shenzhen or FOB Ningbo/Shenzhen; we do not handle DDP, customs clearance, or maintain overseas warehouses. Bulk order lead time is 30-45 days, MOQ from 100 units for stock sizes, 500 units for full custom mold.

Common Buyer Considerations

  • Sample first: $50-200 per sample, 7-10 day lead time, refundable against bulk order.
  • Tooling cost: $3,000-25,000 for new mold, amortized over 3-5 years or 5,000+ units.
  • Compliance: ISO 9001:2015, CE, RoHS 3, REACH SVHC, FCC, UN38.3, IP67/IP68 testing via SGS partner lab.
  • Trade terms: EXW Shenzhen (buyer arranges pickup) or FOB Ningbo/Shenzhen (we deliver to port).
