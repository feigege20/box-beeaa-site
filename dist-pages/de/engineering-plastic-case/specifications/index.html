Frequently Asked Questions

  1. What is PP+GF material? PP+GF (Polypropylene + Glass Fiber) is a composite material with 30% glass fiber reinforcement. Provides 2-3x higher stiffness and impact resistance vs pure PP.
  2. Can you make fire-retardant cases? Yes, our EPC-400 uses UL94 V-0 rated flame-retardant PP. Suitable for electronics, EV battery, and industrial applications requiring fire safety.
  3. What's the minimum order for custom colors? Custom Pantone color matching requires MOQ 1,000 pieces. Standard colors (black, gray, yellow, red, blue) have no MOQ.
  4. Are your cases suitable for NEV battery packs? Yes, our EPC-500 NEV battery case is designed for electric vehicle battery modules with EMI shielding, fire retardancy, and IP67 protection.
